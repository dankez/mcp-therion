Yes, in principle it can be used that way. If you will not enter any 
wall symbols, your drawing will not be clipped and you can draw anything 
than.

You will be just limited by underground symbols. Until now, we used 
therion only for generation of surface contour maps - no other features 
are supported. But we can change this.
