Hi Johny,

there are several tricks for very mazy caves:

1. make short scraps

2. try not to join scraps where passages are joining

3. join named lines (with ID)

4. you may join not only ends of lines. You may use any line point of  
any object not only wall. If a passage begins among blocks you may  
join the beginning of passage walls to blocks line points so they  
will be transformed together.

for example:

join ms3_S1_2:32 js1_S1_2:3

where ms3_S1_2:32 means 32. line point from beginning of line  
ms3_S1_2 and js1_S1_2:3 means 3. line point from beginning of line  
js1_S1_2

5. use transparent pits, overhangs, steps, - check examples on wiki.

line overhang -outline in -clip off

line pit -close on -outline in -clip off

6. use invisible lines if necessary

line border -visibility off -outline in

7. very important if you want to generate an atlas - you should  
organize the structure of maps according to atlas pages and under and  
over laying passages.
for example:

survey xxxxx

....
....
....


map hlavny_tah.m
   js2_S1
   mb1b_s1
   mb1a_s1
   mb2_s1
   ms1_S1
   ms2_S1
   ms2_S2
   break
   ms2_S3
   break
   ms2_S4
   ph1_S1
   ph1_S2
   ph1_S3
   ph1_S4
   ph1_S5
   ph1_S6
   ph1_S7
   ph1_S8
   js1_S1

   preview above kralova_chodba.m
