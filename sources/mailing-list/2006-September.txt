I apologize myself - it is not the problem of joining of three scraps.

the problem is you divide the wall lines and therion is looking for  
the nearest point - which makes the triangle. BTW - the orientation  
of the wall lines is reverse - the yellow tick line should be inside  
the free space - inside the gallery.

The is missing the join command - as opposite to split line command  
in xtherion.

I!ll send you th2 and map file to see the result. There are 2  
pictures too to show the problem.

If you change the subtype of wall you don't need to start with new  
line - just continue with another point and change the subtype back  
to bedrock.

In your case:

I removed all short walls. Then I added points to wall coming from  
bottom and replaced those short walls. From the beginning the this  
line (from the small yellow tick) I added subtypes -
1. point -subtype unsurveyed
2. point -subtype invisible
3. point -subtype unsurveyed
4. point .subtype bedrock

And no the triangle there.

I only hope it is what you want to draw there.

Martin

Yes. The easiest way that really works is to click on maps in "Maps 
structure" toolbox (Compiler) in the order, you want them in legend. 
Each map selected with "select" command will be couloured by different 
colour.

Another possibility is to select just master map like this:

select master_map at survey -map-level 1

Then it will colour sub-maps at level 1 of master_map. This should work 
but I have noticed, there is a bug and one empty map in the legend 
appears in this case.

> How are the vertical co-ordoinates at the ends of
> scraps determined, and is it possible to msake sure they 'join' (have
> the same co-ordinates) both vertically as well as horizontally?

Coordinates are determined by interpolation from stations in scrap. 
Currently we use "inverse distance to a power" as a weighting function.

> option to say 'add vertical lines to complete join' would be useful

There are various conversion filters around which allow you to convert
various wiki markup styles to/from other formats (just google for the
wiki software name and the format you want to convert to to find some).

So you could probably keep documentation in a wiki format so it's easy
for anyone to edit and just find (or even write) filters to convert it
to all the formats you want (or just to something like docbook which can
then be converted to those formats).

After all, wiki markup generally provides most of what you really need
from a markup language, is easier to learn and less intrusive than
docbook, and some wikis allow macros so it can probably be extended
to cover any special requirements.
