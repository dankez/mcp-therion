I have never entered diving data, but I imagine two relatively
straightforward options would be...

Enter the LRUD as a separate block of data
Eg
   data dimensions  station left right up down

Or enter the LRUD with the leg data (assuming that the LRUD is taken at the
'to' station)
Eg
data diving from fromdepth to todepth compass tape left right up down


For drawing completely electronically you could do as Stacho suggests, or
you could set a colour for the map foreground and export the map to pdf,
print it, sketch on paper, scan and trace around it in xtherion. 
Eg
   colour map-fg [97 86 38] # yellow brown
...
   export map -projection plan  \
     -layout Layoutcentreline \
     -output ./Output/CentrelinePlan.pdf  

Bruce
