Paraview:

1. Open VTK file exported from Loch
2. use Filter Calculator:
     (coordsX-XXX000)*iHat+(coordsY-YYYY000)*jHat+coordsZ*kHat 

     Replace XXX000 and YYYY000 with your big part of X and Y coordinates

     Press Apply
3. Save data VTK - ASCII
4. In plain text editor:

Replace part (only numbers!!!):



with part:



5. Open in ParaView and Export Scene as .x3d

6. Open file in MeshLab

7. Filters -> Quality Measure and Computations -> Compute Geometric Measures
